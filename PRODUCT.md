# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS v4 + shadcn/ui, chosen by the user. Frontend only: no backend, CMS, auth, or database. Post content lives in local typed data modules so a real data source can replace it later.

## Users

Other developers reading kryisnn's personal engineering blog, usually arriving from a link or search to read one post in depth, sometimes browsing the archive for related posts. They read on desktop, tablet, and phone.

## Product Purpose

A personal dev / engineering blog published under the handle `kryisnn`: long-form posts about building software, tools, and homelab infrastructure. Success means a reader finishes a post, finds the next one, and comes back.

## Positioning

A first-person record from one engineer who builds and runs their own tools and infrastructure. *(Inferred from the confirmed subject; refine with the real author voice.)*

## Operating Context

Posts mix prose, code, images, and embedded videos from public sources. Media must load lazily behind skeleton placeholders so text is readable immediately on slow connections.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home / archive listing, post reading view, topic filtering, about page.
- Images and embedded videos come from public sources, lazy-loaded with skeleton states.
- Responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- shadcn/ui components used heavily.
- Undecided: real post content, comments, newsletter, RSS, analytics.

## Brand Commitments

- Name / handle: `kryisnn`.
- Binding from the user: minimalist aesthetic, no loud colors.

## Evidence on Hand

No real posts, photos, bio, or social proof exist yet. All post copy is synthetic sample content and must be replaced with real writing; do not invent employers, followers, metrics, or testimonials. Public media is credited to its source.

## Product Principles

1. Reading comes first: the text is usable before any media finishes loading.
2. One author's voice; no marketing tone.
3. Quiet by default; nothing competes with the post.
4. Every page works the same way on a phone as on a desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA baseline: keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion respected, captions/titles on embeds, alt text on images.
